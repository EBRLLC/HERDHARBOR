HerdHarbor Alpha v1.4.5

Rabbit Genetics Prediction, pairing selector fixes, Help Center access, and a public website update focused on making Pair Analysis useful even when breeders do not know every hidden allele.

Better color predictions

  • Actual breeder-readable offspring color ranges when carrier status is unresolved.
  • Phenotype-constrained unknown alleles instead of treating “_” as every allele or stopping the calculation.
  • A/B/C/D/E inheritance plus Broken En and Vienna V/v.
  • Harlequin and Magpie constraints that respect visible Japanese-pattern expression.

Better genetics evidence

  • Known / Direct, Proven by Phenotype, Proven by Offspring, Strongly Inferred, Possible, and Unknown evidence levels.
  • Pedigree evidence weakens through unproven generations instead of becoming automatic proof.
  • Mate-aware offspring evidence and sibling/family evidence.
  • Genetic contradiction warnings without silently rewriting breeder records.

Pairing fix

  • Buck lists now use exact Male matching.
  • Doe lists now use exact Female matching.
  • The former substring error where “Female” could match “male” is blocked by a shared sex-normalization helper.
  • Archived, deceased, sold, and ancestor-only rabbits remain ineligible for active Pair Analysis.

Help & release delivery

  • New “?” Help control inside the app opens the HerdHarbor How-To Center.
  • New Rabbit Genetics & Pair Analysis How-To guide.
  • PWA cache/version refresh for installed testers.
  • Android release metadata updated to version 1.4.5.
Important: HerdHarbor genetics predictions are breeding-planning estimates based on the recorded information and deterministic inheritance rules. They are not DNA test results. Variable traits such as visible Vienna marking and rufus intensity are not assigned unsupported precision.
