Farm management with rabbit-management depth

HerdHarbor brings animal records, breeding, litters, health, pedigrees, genetics, tasks, production, customers, sales, reporting, and account tools into one connected farm-management application. Rabbit management remains HerdHarbor’s strongest specialty, particularly breeding, litter, pedigree, and genetics workflows.

What’s included

  • Animal profiles, identification, status, photos, parentage, and history.
  • Breeding, pregnancy, birth, litter, offspring, and weaning workflows.
  • Rabbit-focused pedigree and genetics tools with selected multi-species support.
  • Health records, measurements, follow-ups, and educational health references.
  • Tasks and recurring work for day-to-day farm management.
  • Production, budgeting, profitability, analytics, and reporting.
  • Customers, reservations, sales, payments, transfers, invoices, receipts, and printable records.
  • Protected cloud sync, cross-device account handling, offline recovery, backups, and app-update safeguards.
  • Subscription billing, referral rewards, and Member-month credits.

Start with 1 month of Member free

Eligible adult accounts receive one calendar month of Member access free, with no credit card required to start. Member is $14.99/month after the free month if you choose to continue. Free Adult is not a signup plan; it is an automatic record-protection fallback for an existing adult Member account if paid Member access later ends because the subscription is canceled or a payment failure interrupts access.

Referral rewards

Share your HerdHarbor Referral ID with other farm owners and breeders. A referral becomes qualified after the referred Member completes their first successful monthly renewal.

Every 5 qualified referrals earns 1 stackable free Member-month credit.

AI-assisted tools

Paper Pedigree photo reading, voice-assisted entry, and photo-assisted entry are coming soon through future public releases. They are not presented as generally available until their separate release decisions are complete.

Built for safer updates and connected use

The stable release strengthens cloud-sync safeguards, cross-device account boundaries, app-update behavior, authentication reliability, subscription handling, monitoring, and regression coverage. Existing records remain protected across supported account and subscription transitions.
