Current Alpha release

HerdHarbor Alpha v1.7.0

Optional standards and judging tools now go further: HerdHarbor surfaces useful public ARBA references with official-source links, adds Kentucky 4-H and configurable FFA/youth-show guidance inside Shows, and keeps all of it optional for breeders who do not use those programs.

ARBA standards registry

  • Structured reference for 53 recognized rabbit breeds.
  • Recognized/public variety references and working-standard entries remain separate.
  • 4-class and 6-class structures and public weight information are included where verified.
  • Unknown or incomplete rules are left unresolved instead of guessed.

More useful ARBA reference

  • HerdHarbor now shows the public information it can verify instead of stopping at a purchase warning.
  • Breed pages surface classification, public maximum weight, public variety/group coverage, and current working-standard counts.
  • Official ARBA links are provided for recognized breeds, Standards Committee material, show rules, showmanship, and 2026–2030 corrections.
  • Official corrections affecting a breed are surfaced directly in its HerdHarbor reference.

Standards browser

  • Browse Rabbit → Breed → Variety without leaving HerdHarbor.
  • Review class structure, public weight references, variety status, physical-characteristic summaries, and breed considerations.
  • Search structured breeder references inside the app.
  • Detailed copyrighted judging prose is not reproduced; official source links remain available when exact controlling language is needed.

Evaluate Against Standard

  • Use recorded age, sex, latest weight, variety/color, supported measurements, and breeder-recorded concerns.
  • See weight status, age-class eligibility, variety status, possible faults or disqualifications, and missing measurements.
  • Saved evaluations become part of the animal’s HerdHarbor history.
  • Every result is labeled as an informational breeder tool.

Kentucky 4-H inside Shows

  • A new Youth Guides area summarizes the public Kentucky State Fair 4-H Rabbit Division guide.
  • Entry, tattoo, check-in, health, travel-cage, substitution, and show-day reminders are available beside show records.
  • Junior and Senior showmanship classes, breed-level awards, Best 4-Class, Best 6-Class, Best in Show, and Reserve Best in Show are referenced from the public guide.
  • HerdHarbor also includes a practical pre-show checklist and direct official-source links.

FFA & local youth shows

  • HerdHarbor does not pretend there is one national FFA rabbit rulebook when actual requirements vary by fair or program.
  • Users can save a local youth-show profile with organization, state, official rules link, eligibility notes, class notes, showmanship notes, and verification date.
  • Kentucky FFA State Fair exhibit resources and relevant public ARBA fair-show rules are linked as starting points.
  • The exact event/fair rulebook remains controlling.

Showmanship practice

  • Practice safe handling, transport to the table, posing, identification, terminology, body/fur knowledge, and health observation.
  • Use quick questions covering breed, sex, age class, tattoo, care routines, and project records.
  • The practice guide follows the public ARBA concept that showmanship evaluates the contestant’s handling and knowledge rather than which rabbit is the best specimen.
  • It is a preparation tool, not an official scoring sheet.

Judging & Shows integration

  • Reference judging terminology, faults, disqualifications, show classifications, and breed considerations.
  • Existing Shows workflows remain intact.
  • Optional fields cover standard edition, variety/group, standards observations, leg, points, BOV, BOSV, BOB, BOSB, Best in Show, and Reserve in Show.
  • Judge, class, placement, comments, notes, and awards continue to use the existing Show records.

Genetics + standards intelligence

  • Pairing context can combine genetics, pedigree evidence, show history, and saved standards evaluations.
  • Repeated weight-at-maturity or standards observations can be surfaced alongside genetic compatibility.
  • Evidence sources remain distinct rather than being collapsed into an unsupported composite score.
  • Genetic possibility remains separate from recognized showroom variety status.

Optional by design

  • ARBA tools default off.
  • Youth Guides are reference tools inside Shows and do not require ARBA mode.
  • General animal records, pedigrees, breeding/genetics, health, Analytics, sales, production, finance, and Shows continue independently.
  • HerdHarbor does not claim to replace an ARBA judge, Extension agent, FFA advisor, premium book, or official show rules.
