HerdHarbor Alpha v1.5.0

Shows brings competition, award, exhibitor, and youth-project records into the same HerdHarbor workspace already used for animals, pedigrees, breeding, health, finance, sales, tasks, and genetics.

Shows & competition records

  • County fair, state fair, 4-H, FFA, ARBA, breed-club, rabbit, poultry, livestock, open, youth, and custom show types.
  • Show dates, organization, location, notes, attachments, and archive history.
  • Multiple classes and entries for the same animal at one show.
  • Placements, scores, judge comments, strengths, improvement notes, and show-specific weight/condition.

Awards & history

  • Multiple awards tied to the correct entry/result.
  • Champion, Reserve, Best of Breed, Best in Show, class winner, ribbon, and custom award support.
  • Animal Show History with shows, classes, first places, awards, and championships.
  • Private Exhibitor Achievement History with year/species/animal/organization filtering.

4-H & FFA projects

  • Optional project records inside Shows—not a separate 4-H app.
  • Link a project to an exhibitor, year, and existing HerdHarbor animal.
  • Goals, notes, progress photos, growth, average daily gain, competitions, and chronological project timeline.
  • Printable HerdHarbor Project Record with a clear non-official-record-book disclaimer.

One farm record system

  • No second animal database: Shows references existing HerdHarbor animals.
  • No second budget: show/project income and expenses are normal HerdHarbor Finance transactions.
  • No second health database: project weights, treatments, medications, vaccinations, and observations use existing Health records.
  • Existing protected cloud sync carries Shows records with the rest of the farm state.

Reports & filtering

  • Show & Awards Report with year, exhibitor, animal, species, organization, placement, award, and judge information.
  • Project Record with goals, growth, finance, health, notes, photos, and show activity.
  • Filters for year, show, exhibitor, animal, species, breed, organization, show type, placement, award, and project.
  • Pagination and lazy-loaded images for larger histories.

Rabbit Genetics stays intact

  • Pair Analysis and genetics-on-pedigree features from v1.4.5 remain available.
  • A/B/C/D/E, Broken En, and Vienna inheritance remain unchanged.
  • Phenotype, pedigree, offspring, and direct-genotype evidence remain distinct.
  • Shows is additive and does not reorganize existing breeding or pedigree workflows.
Design principle: Shows is an integrated HerdHarbor record layer, not a second farm-management system. Competition records point back to the same animals, Health records, Finance transactions, and cloud-synced workspace you already use.
