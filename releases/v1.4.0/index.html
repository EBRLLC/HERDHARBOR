What changed

Not a one-off rabbit color calculator.

Alpha v1.4.0 introduces a reusable HerdHarbor Breeding Intelligence Engine. The common engine handles pair selection, pedigree access, evidence, outcomes and history. Species modules can add their own terminology, reproductive timing and validated genetics without rebuilding the entire breeding system.

Evidence model

HerdHarbor shows what it knows—and what it doesn’t.

ConfirmedBreeder/test information or inheritance that establishes the allele.
InferredStrongly supported by recorded offspring or inheritance evidence.
PossibleGenetically possible, but not established by current records.
UnknownNot enough information. HerdHarbor does not guess.

Example

Exact when the genetics are exact. Conditional when they aren’t.

Black buck (Dd) × Blue doe (dd)
Black50%
Blue50%
Why? That 50/50 result is only shown when the supported D-locus genotype is actually known. If the black buck’s second D-locus allele is unknown, HerdHarbor labels Blue as possible and explains that the outcome depends on whether the buck carries dilute.

Data integrity

Breeding intelligence is additive.

Existing animals do not require a genetic profile. Genetics data and saved predictions remain inside the existing protected HerdHarbor farm record and use the existing cloud-sync/recovery workflow. Confirmed genotype conflicts are retained for breeder review rather than overwritten. Genetic-test records are stored as evidence, while free-text lab results are not automatically translated into alleles without breeder review.

Prediction limits

Rabbit color is more complex than five loci.

v1.4.0 deliberately starts with a supported core A/B/C/D/E model. Modifier genes, spotting, silvering, breed-specific expression, incomplete records and color identification can change the visible result. Outcomes affected by unsupported modifiers are presented as broader families or uncertain results instead of a made-up exact answer.

Oregon State University Extension documents A, B, C, D and E as major rabbit coat-color genes and also documents additional genes such as spotting and silvering. Its E-series material includes Ed > Es > E > ej > e, which is reflected in HerdHarbor’s source-backed core model.
