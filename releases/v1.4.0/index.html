What changed

Not a standalone color calculator.

v1.4.0 introduces the HerdHarbor Breeding Intelligence Engine: a species-aware foundation designed to connect animal records, pedigrees, breeding history, offspring outcomes and genetics. Rabbit coat color is the first fully supported prediction module; future species can plug into the same architecture rather than requiring separate breeding systems.

Unknown alleles stay unknown. Exact percentages only appear when the recorded supported genotypes justify an exact Mendelian calculation.

Recorded kits can provide inheritance evidence about previously unknown carrier states. Confirmed information is protected from silent conflicts.

06

Save a snapshot of what was predicted at breeding time. Later discoveries improve future analysis without rewriting the historical prediction.

Evidence model

HerdHarbor tells you what it knows—and what it doesn’t.

ConfirmedBreeder/test information or inheritance that establishes the allele.
InferredStrongly supported by recorded offspring or other inheritance evidence.
PossibleThe genetics can be present, but available records do not establish them.
UnknownThere is not enough information, so HerdHarbor does not guess.

Safety of the prediction

Rabbit color is more complex than five loci.

Alpha v1.4.0 intentionally starts with a supported core A/B/C/D/E model. Modifier genes, spotting, silvering, breed-specific expression and incomplete records can change the visible result. Outcomes affected by unsupported modifiers are shown as broader color families rather than a made-up exact answer.

Oregon State University Extension documents the major A, B, C, D and E coat-color genes and also describes additional spotting and silvering genetics, which is why HerdHarbor does not present its prediction engine as a guarantee. The goal is better breeding information with visible evidence and uncertainty.

Reference basis

Genetics rules are source-backed.

Production rules are being documented against breeder and extension references rather than generated from an AI-only rule list.
