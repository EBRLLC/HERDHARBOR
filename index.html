Why HerdHarbor

Your record system should become more useful as you grow—not harder to afford.

HerdHarbor began after livestock and rabbit breeders voiced the same frustration: useful recordkeeping tools can become expensive or complicated as an operation grows.

The goal is a modern, practical platform built around fast barn-side entry, clear records, user ownership of data, and feedback from the people who raise animals.

Fair pricing remains the goal Growth should be a success, not a pricing penalty.
Tested by real breeders Current features are being shaped by hands-on tester feedback.
Ready for mixed operations One workspace can hold rabbits, poultry, dogs, horses, livestock, and other animals.

Questions

What to know before testing.

What species can I track now?

The current build includes rabbits, chickens, ducks, turkeys, dogs, horses, goats, sheep, cattle, pigs, and an Other category for custom animals and farm products.

Are my records synced?

Yes. The tester build uses protected accounts, record-by-record cloud merging, visible sync status, conflict protection, and an offline device copy. During launch testing, testers should also export regular JSON backups.

What can I import or export?

You can export or restore a complete JSON backup. Excel tools cover Animals, Customers, Sales, Payments, Breeding, Births, Medical, Production, actual Budgeting, and annual plans. You can also print pedigrees, invoices, receipts, bills of sale, QR cards, and focused reports.

How do payments affect my Budget?

An invoice does not count as cash received. Each recorded payment creates one linked Budget income entry, and edits stay synchronized so income is not duplicated or overstated.

How do QR cards and animal transfers work?

Private QR cards open the correct animal after the farm owner signs in. A completed sale can also create a downloadable transfer file containing the animal and a three-generation pedigree for duplicate-safe import into another HerdHarbor account.

How much will HerdHarbor cost?

Final pricing has not been announced. The guiding promise remains fair, transparent pricing without an extreme jump simply because a user has more animals.

Is HerdHarbor finished?

Not yet. Version 0.5.1 is the launch-hardening tester build. Major features are frozen while remaining bugs, account deletion, mobile behavior, synchronization, backups, and offline operation are verified for v1.0 submission.
