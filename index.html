Current release

What changed in Alpha v1.8.1.

v1.8.1 focuses on launch readiness around accounts and subscriptions while keeping HerdHarbor’s established livestock-management engines intact.

Subscriptions are ready for launch

HerdHarbor now has checkout, billing status, cancellation/reactivation, Customer Portal access, paid-entitlement synchronization, and launch-trial enforcement.

Referral rewards are built in

Every eligible account can use a public Referral ID. A referral qualifies after the referred Member’s first successful monthly renewal, and every five qualified referrals earns one Member-month credit.

Safer account registration

New signup includes an adult account-holder age gate, required account attestations, youth-supervision language, profile controls, and additional abuse/fraud safeguards.

Analytics

Turn records into decisions.

HerdHarbor’s analytics layer brings together growth and weight trends, breeding performance, Shows, production, health measurements, sales, revenue, feed and farm-performance views, with market analytics foundations available where supported.

Genetics

Rabbit genetics plus a growing multi-species engine.

Domestic rabbit genetics includes phenotype/genotype tools, pairing intelligence, compatibility handling, pedigree context, and predicted outcomes. HerdHarbor also includes the foundation and UI for supported multi-species genetics workflows.

Optional standards

Use standards as context—not a requirement.

Optional ARBA standards and judging resources can be used alongside Shows, breeding, and animal records. HerdHarbor keeps standards guidance separate from core recordkeeping so users can choose whether it fits their operation.

Questions

What to know right now.

What version is HerdHarbor on?

The current public release is HerdHarbor Alpha v1.8.1.

How much does HerdHarbor cost?

Junior is free. Member is $14.99 per month. Business is still in development. Founder is an internal entitlement and is not a public signup plan.

What happens on October 1, 2026?

The current launch policy provides Member-level trial access through September 30. Beginning October 1, accounts without a paid or other qualifying entitlement fall back to Junior rather than losing their records.

How do referral rewards work?

A referred Member must complete their first successful monthly renewal before the referral qualifies. Every five qualified referrals earns one stackable Member-month credit.

Can HerdHarbor diagnose an animal?

No. HerdHarbor is not a veterinary provider. Health and symptom tools are for recordkeeping and education and do not replace veterinary diagnosis or treatment.

Where are the How-To instructions?

The current How-To Center is available from the navigation or directly at herdharbor.com/how-to/ and covers the major day-to-day workflows.
