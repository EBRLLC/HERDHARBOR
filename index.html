Current release

What changed in Alpha v1.8.4.

v1.8.4 focuses on production stability: cloud-sync rollout safeguards, cross-device integrity, PWA updates, auth protection, subscription reliability, runtime efficiency, and stronger release regression coverage.

Individual engines now work together

Animal profiles, breeding and litters, Health, Tasks, sales, transfers, production, analytics, and reporting share canonical records so work can move through the app as one smoother stream instead of disconnected tools.

Stability where it matters

Cloud sync, cross-device state integrity, PWA updates, authentication, subscription state, offline recovery, and runtime loading have additional safeguards and regression coverage in v1.8.4.

AI-assisted entry is coming soon

Paper Pedigree scanning, voice-assisted entry, and photo-assisted record entry are in testing before wider release. These tools are part of HerdHarbor membership and are not planned as a separate AI add-on charge.

Analytics

Turn records into decisions.

HerdHarbor’s analytics layer brings together growth and weight trends, breeding performance, Shows, production, health measurements, sales, revenue, feed and farm-performance views, with market analytics foundations available where supported.

Genetics

Rabbit genetics plus a growing multi-species engine.

Domestic rabbit genetics includes phenotype/genotype tools, pairing intelligence, compatibility handling, pedigree context, and predicted outcomes. HerdHarbor also includes the foundation and UI for supported multi-species genetics workflows.

Optional standards

Use standards as context—not a requirement.

Optional ARBA standards and judging resources can be used alongside Shows, breeding, and animal records. HerdHarbor keeps standards guidance separate from core recordkeeping so users can choose whether it fits their operation.

Questions

What to know right now.

What version is HerdHarbor on?

The current public release is HerdHarbor Alpha v1.8.4.

How much does HerdHarbor cost?

Free Adult and Junior are free account paths. Member is $14.99 per month after the trial. Eligible adult accounts receive one calendar month of Member free, with no credit card required to start. Business is still in development, and Founder remains an internal entitlement rather than a public signup plan.

How does the free Member month work?

Eligible adult accounts receive one calendar month of Member access free. No credit card is required to start. If Member access later ends, eligible adult accounts fall back to Free Adult without deleting existing herd records.

How do referral rewards work?

Share your Referral ID during signup. A referred Member becomes qualified after their first successful monthly renewal. Every five qualified referrals earns one stackable free Member-month credit.

Can HerdHarbor diagnose an animal?

No. HerdHarbor is not a veterinary provider. Health and symptom tools are for recordkeeping and education and do not replace veterinary diagnosis or treatment.

Where are the How-To instructions?

The current How-To Center is available from the navigation or directly at herdharbor.com/how-to/ and covers the major day-to-day workflows.
