Version 1.3.0 Alpha

HerdHarbor keeps the complete Member recordkeeping workflow while making cattle identification and pedigree records easier to use in day-to-day farm work.

HerdHarbor Business — built for farms with a team.

HerdHarbor Business is being developed for farms and livestock operations that need more than individual recordkeeping. The upcoming Business workspace will add employee access, job assignments, recurring farm tasks, completion tracking, and management controls while keeping animal records, production, health, breeding, and farm operations connected in one system.

Built from farm feedback

Why HerdHarbor Business?

A farm with approximately 10 employees asked HerdHarbor for a better way to assign daily work than text messages and phone-call reminders.

HerdHarbor Business is being designed so owners and managers can assign work once, employees can clearly see what they are responsible for, and completed jobs become part of a visible farm activity history instead of disappearing inside message threads.

01

Separate Business workspace

Business accounts will have a dedicated team-management workspace while retaining HerdHarbor's livestock, production, health, breeding, financial, customer, sales, and recordkeeping tools.

02

Employee accounts

Farm owners will be able to add employees to the operation so workers can sign in with their own access instead of sharing the owner's HerdHarbor account.

03

Role-based permissions

Owners will control what each team member can see and change. Employees can be limited to the records and responsibilities needed for their job without receiving unrestricted access to the entire business.

04

Task assignments

Owners and managers will be able to assign jobs directly to individual employees or members of the farm team with clear instructions, due dates, animals, locations, and priorities attached to the assignment.

05

Recurring farm jobs

Daily, weekly, monthly, and custom recurring assignments will support routine work such as feeding, watering, cleaning, milking, egg collection, health checks, pasture checks, maintenance, breeding preparation, and other repeated farm responsibilities.

06

Employee Today view

Employees will have a simple view showing what needs to be completed today, what is coming next, and anything overdue so workers do not have to search through the full farm-management system to find their assignments.

07

Completion tracking

Assigned work will have visible completion status so owners and managers can see what has been completed, what is still open, and when the work was recorded.

08

Animal-linked jobs

Tasks will be able to reference a specific animal, herd, flock, pen, pasture, or other work area. Employees will know exactly where the job belongs and managers will be able to connect completed work back to the appropriate farm records.

09

Health and treatment assignments

Farms will be able to assign animal checks, follow-up observations, treatment reminders, weighing, medication-related tasks, and other health work while maintaining appropriate access controls around sensitive records.

10

Production assignments

Work such as milking, egg collection, livestock checks, hay work, feeding, weighing, and other production activities can be assigned to employees and connected with HerdHarbor's existing production records.

11

Manager oversight

Managers will be able to review outstanding assignments, completed work, overdue tasks, employee workloads, and the day's farm activity without contacting every employee individually for an update.

12

Farm activity history

Completed assignments will create a clearer operational history showing what work was performed, who completed it, and when it was finished instead of leaving important information scattered across calls and text messages.

Questions

What to know before testing.

What species can I track now?

The current build includes rabbits, chickens, ducks, turkeys, dogs, horses, goats, sheep, cattle, pigs, and an Other category for custom animals and farm products. Cattle records also support dedicated ear-tag number and ear-tag color fields.

Are my records synced?

Yes. The tester build uses protected accounts, record-by-record cloud merging, visible sync status, conflict protection, and an offline device copy. During launch testing, testers should also export regular JSON backups.

What can I import or export?

You can export or restore a complete JSON backup. Excel tools cover Animals, Customers, Sales, Payments, Breeding, Births, Medical, Production, actual Budgeting, annual plans, and cattle ear-tag fields. You can also print pedigrees, invoices, receipts, bills of sale, QR cards, and focused reports.

Can the symptom guide diagnose or treat an animal?

No. HerdHarbor is not a veterinary provider. The guide is a static educational reference that shows possible concerns and urgency, not a diagnosis or treatment. Contact a licensed veterinarian for every health concern and seek emergency veterinary help for red-flag signs.

How do payments affect my Budget?

An invoice does not count as cash received. Each recorded payment creates one linked Budget income entry, and edits stay synchronized so income is not duplicated or overstated.

How do QR cards and animal transfers work?

Private QR cards open the correct animal after the farm owner signs in. A completed sale can also create a downloadable transfer file containing the animal and a three-generation pedigree for duplicate-safe import into another HerdHarbor account. Supported cattle ear-tag information is included with the animal record.

How much will HerdHarbor cost?

Final pricing has not been announced on this page. The guiding promise remains fair, transparent pricing without an extreme jump simply because a user has more animals.

Is HerdHarbor finished?

HerdHarbor is now at Alpha v1.3.0. The current release preserves the complete Member experience while adding dedicated cattle ear-tag records throughout the workflow and improving pedigree identification with clearer visual cards and optional stored-animal photos. The app remains in active alpha testing as feedback continues to shape future releases.
