New in the Alpha

Breeding decisions backed by real rabbit genetics.

HerdHarbor's Rabbit Genetics system goes beyond showing the same generic color list for every pairing. The genetics engine analyzes the sire and dam using recorded phenotype, known genotype, pedigree evidence, and offspring evidence to calculate genetically possible outcomes while preserving uncertainty where an allele is still unknown.

01

A/B/C/D/E inheritance

Track the five core rabbit color loci and calculate inheritance from the actual alleles available from each parent instead of relying only on visible color names.

02

Broken-pattern genetics

En and en are included in pair analysis so broken, solid, and Charlie-pattern possibilities can be evaluated separately from the base coat-color loci.

03

Vienna built in

HerdHarbor supports Vienna Carrier (VC), Vienna Marked (VM), and Blue-Eyed White (BEW) inheritance so breeders can account for the Vienna gene when evaluating a pairing.

04

Pedigree carrier inference

Known colors and genetics in the pedigree can identify recessive alleles that may have traveled through earlier generations instead of treating every visually identical rabbit as genetically identical.

05

Offspring evidence

Previous litters can provide evidence of hidden recessive genes. HerdHarbor can use genetically informative offspring to narrow a parent's possible genotype as more breeding history is recorded.

06

Uncertainty stays visible

HerdHarbor does not silently turn an unknown allele into a known one. Unknown, possible, inferred, and supported genetics remain distinct so breeders can see how strong the evidence behind a prediction really is.

Why it matters

Same visible color does not always mean same genetics.

A black rabbit may carry chocolate, dilute, chinchilla, Japanese, non-extension, Vienna, or other recessive genetics without showing them visually. HerdHarbor is being built to preserve that difference and make pair analysis more useful as pedigree and litter history grow.

Genetics predictions are breeding-planning tools, not DNA tests. When a genotype is incomplete, HerdHarbor keeps the uncertainty visible rather than presenting an unsupported result as certain.

How does HerdHarbor predict rabbit colors?

HerdHarbor's Rabbit Genetics system analyzes the sire and dam using the A, B, C, D, and E color loci together with En broken-pattern inheritance and Vienna inheritance. When available, pedigree and offspring evidence can help identify possible hidden recessive alleles. If part of a genotype is still unknown, HerdHarbor keeps that uncertainty visible rather than pretending the allele is known.

Does HerdHarbor support Vienna carriers and Vienna-marked rabbits?

Yes. Rabbit Genetics v2 includes Vienna inheritance and supports recorded Vienna Carrier (VC), Vienna Marked (VM), and Blue-Eyed White (BEW) status during breeding analysis.
