Analytics

Turn records into decisions.

HerdHarbor’s analytics layer brings together growth and weight trends, breeding performance, Shows, production, health measurements, sales, revenue, feed and farm-performance views, with market analytics foundations available where supported.

Genetics

Rabbit genetics plus growing multi-species support.

Domestic rabbit genetics includes phenotype/genotype tools, pairing guidance, compatibility handling, pedigree context, and predicted outcomes. HerdHarbor also includes support for selected multi-species genetics workflows.

Optional standards

Use standards as context—not a requirement.

Optional ARBA standards and judging resources can be used alongside Shows, breeding, and animal records. HerdHarbor keeps standards guidance separate from core recordkeeping so users can choose whether it fits their operation.

Questions

What to know right now.

Where can I use HerdHarbor?

HerdHarbor is live on the web at app.herdharbor.com and can be installed as a web app on supported mobile and desktop devices. Google Play and the Apple App Store are coming soon.

How much does HerdHarbor cost?

Junior is the selectable free account option. Member is $14.99 per month after the trial, and eligible adult accounts receive one calendar month of Member free with no credit card required to start. Free Adult is not a signup plan or pricing tier; it is an automatic fallback used to preserve an eligible adult Member account's existing animals and records if Member access later ends. Business is still in development, and Founder pricing is reserved for eligible Founder accounts rather than offered as a public signup plan.

How does the free Member month work?

Eligible adult accounts receive one calendar month of Member access free. No credit card is required to start. Free Adult is not something you choose after the trial. It is an automatic safety fallback that preserves existing animals and records if eligible adult Member access later ends because of cancellation, non-renewal, or billing failure.

How do referral rewards work?

Share your Referral ID during signup. A referred Member becomes qualified after their first successful monthly renewal. Every five qualified referrals earns one stackable free Member-month credit.

Can HerdHarbor diagnose an animal?

No. HerdHarbor is not a veterinary provider. Health and symptom tools are for recordkeeping and education and do not replace veterinary diagnosis or treatment.

Where are the How-To instructions?

The current How-To Center is available from the navigation or directly at herdharbor.com/how-to/ and covers the major day-to-day workflows.
