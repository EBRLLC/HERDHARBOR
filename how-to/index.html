Pedigrees

How to Create a Pedigree

HerdHarbor builds pedigrees from the sire and dam relationships stored on animal records. Connecting the parents allows the app to follow the family line into earlier generations when those records are available.

  1. Open Animals. Find and open the animal whose pedigree you want to build or review.
  2. Open the animal's pedigree information. Go to the Pedigree area and review the sire and dam fields.
  3. Connect the sire and dam. Select the correct parent records when they already exist in HerdHarbor.
  4. Add missing ancestry where needed. Enter available identifying information for parents or earlier ancestors that are not already stored.
  5. Review the generations. Confirm names, sex, identification or registration information, color, breeder information, dates of birth, and parent relationships.
  6. Choose your pedigree appearance. When available, use Off, Compact, or Visual photo mode. Stored animal profile photos can be used in visual pedigrees.
  7. Print or export after reviewing. Check the full pedigree first, then use the available print or export option.
Tip: If an ancestor appears in the wrong place, review the sire and dam connections on the affected animal records first.

Breeding & Litters

How to Enter a New Litter

Use a birth or litter record to keep the parents, birth date, number born, outcomes, and offspring connected.

  1. Open Breeding, Births & Litters. Choose the option to add a new birth or litter record.
  2. Select the dam. Choose the female that gave birth. Use the existing breeding record when available.
  3. Confirm the sire. Select the sire when known so offspring pedigrees can populate correctly.
  4. Enter the birth date and litter totals. Record the date and outcomes such as born alive, stillborn, fostered, or lost.
  5. Add useful notes. Record litter size, delivery notes, foster information, dam performance, or other observations.
  6. Create individual offspring records when needed. Add names, tags or identification, sex, and other known details.
  7. Verify the links. Confirm offspring show the correct sire, dam, and birth record.
Tip: Creating offspring from the litter workflow helps keep the birth record and pedigree connections together.

Health & Medical

How to Add Medical Data

Keep health observations, treatments, medications, vaccinations, and veterinary notes with the animal they belong to.

  1. Open the animal record. Go to Animals and select the animal whose health information you need to record.
  2. Open Health or Medical records. Choose the health/medical area and start a new record.
  3. Enter the date. Use the date the observation, treatment, vaccination, or veterinary visit occurred.
  4. Record what happened. Describe the observation, illness, injury, routine care, vaccination, or other event.
  5. Add treatment or medication details. Record the treatment or medication name, dosage or amount, route or method, and follow-up information when applicable.
  6. Add veterinary or follow-up notes. Include relevant instructions, recheck dates, withdrawal information, or other notes.
  7. Save and review the history. Confirm the entry appears in the correct animal's health history.
Important: HerdHarbor is a recordkeeping tool. Its educational symptom guide is not veterinary advice, a diagnosis, or a substitute for a licensed veterinarian.
